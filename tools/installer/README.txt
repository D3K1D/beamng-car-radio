CarRadio - play any program from your PC through your car in BeamNG.drive
==========================================================================

1. Start "CarRadio Companion" (Start menu). Play your music in Spotify, Chrome,
   or any other program, press the refresh button, pick that program and press Start.
2. In BeamNG.drive: pause menu > UI Apps > add "CarRadio". The music now comes from
   the car you're driving, in 3D. It keeps playing in menus and on the map.
3. While the game is connected, the companion turns the program down on your own
   speakers (to about -60 dB) so you only hear it from the car. Its volume comes back
   when you stop, quit the companion, or leave the game.

The bar in the game:
  |<<  >||  >>   previous / play-pause / next for the captured program (like media keys)
  slider         your car's volume (up to 1000% for very quiet sources; double-click = 100%)
  B              bass boost; loud bass shakes your car
  gear           settings window: now playing, other cars' volume, muffling, damage
                 effects, ULTRA BASS BOOST (5x shake)

Multiplayer (BeamMP): on servers that run CarRadio, the mod is downloaded when you
join, and you hear other players' car radios at their cars, at the volume and bass
their owner set. Keep the companion running. The settings window shows the status.

Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps > CarRadio.
Sharing captured music with other players is meant for private groups of friends.
