CarRadio - play any program from your PC through your car in BeamNG.drive
==========================================================================

1. Start "CarRadio Companion" (Start menu). Play your music in Spotify, Chrome,
   or any other program, press the refresh button, pick that program and press Start.
2. In BeamNG.drive: pause menu > UI Apps > add "CarRadio". Press the play button
   in the app. The music now comes from your car, in 3D.
3. While the game is connected, the companion turns the program down on your own
   speakers so you only hear it from the car. Its volume returns when you stop,
   quit the companion, or leave the game.

Multiplayer (BeamMP): on servers that run CarRadio, the mod is downloaded when you
join, and you hear other players' car radios at their cars. Keep the companion
running. The app's settings (gear icon) show the multiplayer status.

Settings in the game app:
  - Volume slider (up to 1000% for very quiet sources; double-click for 100%)
  - B: bass boost. Loud bass shakes your car; ULTRA BASS BOOST multiplies that by 5.
  - Other cars' volume, damage effects, muffling outside the car.

Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps > CarRadio.
Sharing captured music with other players is meant for private groups of friends.
